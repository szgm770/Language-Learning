# English A0 for Hebrew speakers – "before you start"

Three short units for people who know no English at all. They come before A1 (`en-a1.md`).
Content is Torah-observant friendly like the rest of the course (see CLAUDE.md).

| # | Unit | What the learner can do afterwards | Content |
|---|------|------------------------------------|---------|
| 1 | `u00a-letters` – The English alphabet | name and recognise all 26 letters (capital and small), hear the first sound of a word, spell Noa / Ben / Eli | one lexeme per letter (`l-a` … `l-z`), spelling with word-bank tiles |
| 2 | `u00b-sounds` – The sounds of English | tell apart the sounds that Hebrew speakers merge: th, w/v, r, short vs. long vowels, sh / ch / j, silent e | one lexeme per sound (`s-…`), listening minimal pairs |
| 3 | `u00c-words` – Words you already know | read and write ~22 international words (banana, internet, tennis, tourist …) | ordinary lexemes; ends with `a0-test` |

Rules specific to A0
- Letters and sounds are lexemes too, so they enter the review deck like words.
- Prompts and tips are in Hebrew; options are English words or letters.
- Do not reuse a word that A1 teaches (lexeme ids are unique across the course): A0 words are only those that A1–B2 never need.
